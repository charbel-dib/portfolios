About

I trained as a journalist

I studied Mass Communication and Journalism, and I still work the way a reporter does. Before I start filming a product I look for what is actually interesting about it, which is usually what someone does with it rather than the product sitting on a table.

I write and caption in both Arabic and English. Most brands here need both in the same cut, so I build the overlays that way from the start instead of re-editing later.
